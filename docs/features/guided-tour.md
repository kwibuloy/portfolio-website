# Guided tour

Decided 2026-10-14. A cursor with a chat bubble that tours the home page, written as Derick showing a visitor around.

- **When**: on a visitor's first visit to `/`, 1.5 s after load. Remembered with `localStorage` (`tour-seen`, set when it starts). "Take the tour" in the sidebar replays it, going to `/` first if needed. Leaving `/` ends it.
- **Steps** (`src/content/tour.ts`): greeting at the portrait, then (2026-10-14) the pointer moves to the Skip button and says how to end the tour (Esc, Skip, or scrolling or clicking; a step can have a `touchText` for touch screens, where there is no Esc), then each social link in the intro, each sidebar nav link, then "Ask anything". Casual first person. Targets are found by `data-tour="…"` attributes; the visible copy is used, since the sidebar is rendered twice.
- **Pace**: plays through on its own (typed text, then a pause to read). Faster since 2026-10-15: 1 s start delay, 18 ms per character, 0.9 s + 8 ms per character to read, glides of 0.35 to 0.8 s. The greeting cannot be interrupted (2026-10-15): the "Skip tour" button appears as the pointer heads to it in step 2, and only from then on do Skip, Esc and visitor input end the tour. The button shows at every size, from that step until the tour stops (it goes the moment the tour is stopped, not after the pointer fades) (with an Esc hint where there is a mouse), and any scroll, click, tap or key press ends the tour, so it never fights a visitor who wants to look around (decided 2026-10-14, pre-deploy audit). A first-visit tour that has not started yet is also called off by any of those. Below `lg` the tour opens the mobile drawer (non-modal, via the `tour:drawer` window event) for the nav steps and closes it at the end. When a step scrolls its target into view, the tour waits for the scroll to finish (`scrollend`, or 150 ms without a scroll event, at most 3 s) before aiming the pointer, so a replay from the bottom of the page still lands on the portrait (2026-10-15).
- **Animation**: GSAP glides the pointer, with different x and y eases so the path bends like a hand moving a mouse. Motion pops the bubble in and out. The bubble never covers its target (`placeBubble`, 2026-10-14): it sits just below the whole target when there is room, otherwise just above it (as on the Skip button); sideways it starts right of the pointer, flips to end at it near the right edge, and stays on screen. The portrait step keeps the original placement instead, hanging right under the pointer (`bubbleAt: "pointer"` in `tour.ts`), since clearing the whole photo would put the bubble far away. The pointer is layered above the Skip button. No box or outline is drawn on the target; instead its own hover animations play. The target gets `data-tour-active`, which the `hover:` variant in `globals.css` also matches (so `hover:` and `group-hover:` classes apply), and the tour sends `pointerout`/`pointerover` so React-driven hovers (the nav's sliding pill) follow. The pointer stays still on each target; a single `pointermove` where it rests lets the portrait's layers settle toward it.
- **Accessibility**: the full line of each step goes to an `aria-live` region; the pointer and bubble are `aria-hidden`. Focus is never trapped. Reduced motion: the pointer jumps and text appears at once.
- **Code**: `src/components/ui/tour/guided-tour.tsx`, loaded by `tour-loader.tsx` (in the root layout) only on `/` or when "Take the tour" is pressed, so GSAP does not ship to the other pages. A request from another page is held in `src/lib/tour.ts` (`startTour` / `takeTourRequest`) until the tour is on `/` to take it.
